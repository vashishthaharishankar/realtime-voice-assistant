# Kotak Mahindra Prime — Voice Assistant Knowledge Summary

Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited (KMPL) is a subsidiary of Kotak Mahindra Bank and specializes in automobile finance in India.

## Products
- New Car Finance: funding up to 100% for eligible models, tenure 12–84 months.
- Used Car Finance: up to 90% funding, segments include pre-approved, preferred, and salary-linked options.
- Car Loan Takeover / Refinance: transfer existing loan; top-up possible based on track record.
- Cash Against Car: loan against lien-free owned vehicle with KMPL marking lien.

## New Car Schemes
- Margin Money: up to 100% invoice funding for select models.
- Step Up: EMIs increase periodically for customers expecting income growth.
- Balloon / Low EMI: 10–25% paid as final EMI.
- Advance EMI: pay initial EMIs upfront to reduce later burden.

## Application Channels
- Website: primeloans.kotak.com
- SMS: Text APPLY to 5676788
- Track application via prospect number or registered mobile on the website.

## Processing
- New car: typically within 48 hours after complete documentation.
- Used car: up to 72 hours after documentation.

## Eligibility (illustrative)
- Salaried: generally 21+ years, income and age-at-maturity checks apply.
- Self-employed: business vintage and financials required.
- Credit decision solely at discretion of KMPL; RBI guidelines apply.

## Charges
- Processing fee up to ~3% of loan amount (illustrative).
- RTO, NACH, stamp duty, prepayment charges may apply as per policy.

## Grievance
Customers may raise grievances through KMPL website grievance redressal channels.
For branch locations, refer to the official branch list document in knowledge base.

## Important
Never promise approval, rates, or EMI without system verification.
KMPL serves car finance only—not personal loans, credit cards, or bike loans.
